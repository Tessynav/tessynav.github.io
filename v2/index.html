Tesla connection

Connected to your Tesla.

Sign in with your Tesla account once, and TessyNav and your car work as a team.

  • Sync from car: the trip already set in your car opens in TessyNav: destination, time and battery at arrival
  • Send to car: pick a place in TessyNav and it goes into the car's own navigation, so it plans Superchargers and preheats the battery
  • Signs in on Tesla's own page: TessyNav never sees your password
  • Nothing is read in the background, only when you tap
Spotify

Your Tesla's Spotify, right on the map.

The song playing in your car sits beside your speed. Skip, pause, search and pick a playlist without ever leaving the map. It plays straight on the car's own Spotify.

  • Now playing, with previous, play/pause and next
  • Search songs, artists and playlists
  • Your playlists and recently played
  • Plays on the car's speakers through Spotify Connect

Needs a Spotify Premium account.
