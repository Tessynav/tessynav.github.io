Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Last updated November 18, 2026 (waitlist added)
The short version. TessyNav runs in your Tesla's web browser. It has no user accounts and no advertising or analytics trackers. Your settings, saved places and learned routes stay in your car's browser. Some information leaves the car only to make a feature work (for example your position to calculate a route) or when you choose to send it (a report, a trip recording). We never sell personal information.

1. Who we are

TessyNav is a navigation web app made by Mazen Accad Wattar, an individual in Montréal, Québec, Canada ("TessyNav", "we").

2. Person in charge of the protection of personal information

Mazen Accad Wattar, Founder · privacy@tessynav.com. We answer requests within 30 days.

4. Location and route learning are off by default

TessyNav asks for your location only when you open the map, and your browser asks for your permission first. "Learn my usual routes" stays off until you turn it on in Settings. You can turn either off at any time.

5. Service providers and information outside Québec

To work, TessyNav uses these services. They are located outside Québec (mostly in the United States or Europe); before using them we assessed the privacy risks, and each receives only what its feature needs:

  • GitHub (hosting of the app, map data, reports and encrypted recordings) · Cloudflare (our server, email)
  • OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap, Overpass (map data) · Mapbox, OSRM (routes and traffic) · Photon (Komoot) (address search)
  • TomTom (traffic incidents) · Open-Meteo (weather) · Google Firebase (shared road alerts)
  • Tesla and Spotify, only if you connect them · jsDelivr / unpkg (app libraries)

Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and browser details when the app contacts them.

6. Cookies and trackers

We use no advertising or analytics trackers. The app stores your settings in your browser's local storage and keeps map files in its cache so it works with a weak signal.

7. How long we keep information

  • Information in your browser: until you clear it, or use the buttons on the Account page.
  • Road alerts: shown to drivers for 30 minutes to 4 hours, then kept at most 1 year.
  • Reports and trip recordings: as long as needed to fix the problem, and at most 2 years.
  • Waitlist sign-ups: until you unsubscribe, and at most 1 year after launch.
  • Payment records (when Premium exists): as long as tax law requires.

8. Security and incidents

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), trip recordings are encrypted before they leave the car, and our secret keys (for Tesla and our storage) stay on our server, never in the app. If an incident creates a risk of serious injury, we will notify the people affected and the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access or correct your information, withdraw your consent, get a copy of the information you provided, or have it deleted. Write to privacy@tessynav.com. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

10. Children

TessyNav is made for drivers and is not intended for anyone under 14.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date, and show a notice in the app for important changes.
