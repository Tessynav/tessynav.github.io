MapKoon + Spotify

Create your own free Spotify key on this phone, then send it to your car with one tap. About 5 minutes, once. You need Spotify Premium.

1

Open the Spotify developer page

Log in with the Spotify account you use in your Tesla.

Open Spotify for Developers

First time? Spotify asks you to accept the Developer Terms (scroll up if you don't see the box) and may ask you to verify your email.

2

Tap “Create app”

It's at the top right of the Dashboard. Fill in:

App nameMapKoon
App descriptionMusic controls for my Tesla
Redirect URIthe address below, then tap Add
Which API/SDKstick Web API only
https://app.mapkoon.com/v2/

Can't see “Create app”? In your browser menu (⋮), turn on Desktop site and reload.

3

Save, then copy your Client ID

Agree to the terms and tap Save. Open your new app, then Settings, and copy the Client ID (32 letters and numbers).

Never copy the Client Secret. MapKoon doesn't need it, and nobody should ask you for it.

4

Send it to your car

Paste the Client ID here. The car code is filled in when you scan the car's QR; otherwise type the 6 letters shown in the car (Connect Spotify → Use my own Spotify key).

The car fills it in by itself and opens Spotify's login: tap Agree there. The code works for 10 minutes and only once.

Something not working?

I don't see “Create app”

Turn on Desktop site in your browser menu, or use a computer. The button only appears for Premium accounts (Family and Duo count). Spotify also sometimes pauses new apps for everyone; if so, try again later.

I don't see the “Accept” button

Scroll up: on phones the terms box can be squeezed under the purple bar. Desktop site helps too.

Spotify says the redirect URI is invalid

Use the Copy button above. It must be exactly https://app.mapkoon.com/v2/, with the slash at the end and no spaces.

“No Spotify player found” in MapKoon

Open Spotify on the car once (Music → Spotify) and play anything, then try again.

Can my family use it?

Yes: in your Spotify app → User Management, add their Spotify emails (up to 5 people). Or each person creates their own key with this page.

Your key stays in your car's browser. MapKoon never sees your Spotify password: you log in on Spotify's own page.
